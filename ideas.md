# Design Ideas — 144 Scranton Connector Offering Website

## Context
A high-end commercial real estate investment offering site for a 10-acre institutional property in Brunswick, GA. The audience is sophisticated investors, developers, healthcare systems, and government entities. The tone must be authoritative, data-driven, and aspirational — conveying the property's potential rather than its current state.

## Idea 1: "Coastal Cartographic" — Maritime Data Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Cartographic modernism meets coastal industrial — inspired by nautical charts, port authority documents, and the Golden Isles' maritime heritage.

**Core Principles:**
1. Data as design — charts, maps, and statistics are the visual centerpiece, not decoration
2. Restrained luxury — dark navy and aged parchment tones with precise gold accents
3. Horizontal layering — sections feel like stacked map overlays, each revealing new information
4. Typography as authority — serif headlines command attention; monospace data labels feel precise

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#1B2A4A) as the primary background for hero sections; warm off-white (#F5F0E8) parchment for content sections; gold (#C9A84C) for accents and data callouts. This palette evokes nautical charts and investment-grade documents simultaneously.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed hero with satellite aerial overlay and coordinate data. Subsequent sections use an asymmetric 60/40 split — text left, data visualization right. A persistent left-rail navigation anchors the user.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Coordinate grid overlay on hero image (subtle, like a nautical chart)
2. Gold horizontal rules separating data sections
3. Monospace font for all numbers and statistics

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth scroll with section-by-section reveal animations. Map sections use parallax depth. Hover states on data cards reveal additional detail.

**Animation:** Sections fade up on scroll entry. Numbers count up when they enter the viewport. Map pins drop with a subtle bounce.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (bold) for headlines; Source Serif Pro for body; JetBrains Mono for data/statistics.

## Idea 2: "Brutalist Opportunity" — Bold Investment Thesis Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Editorial brutalism meets investment banking — inspired by Goldman Sachs pitch decks and The Economist magazine layouts.

**Core Principles:**
1. Confidence through contrast — stark black and white with a single electric accent color
2. Typography-first — oversized headlines are the primary visual element
3. Grid discipline — strict 12-column grid with deliberate rule-breaking for emphasis
4. Scarcity messaging — white space is used aggressively to convey premium positioning

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#111111) and pure white (#FFFFFF) as the base; PIER orange (#CB521E) as the sole accent color for CTAs, highlights, and data callouts. This palette signals institutional authority and PIER brand alignment.

**Layout Paradigm:** Oversized typographic hero with a single large number (10 ACRES) as the visual anchor. Content sections alternate between full-bleed dark and white backgrounds. A sticky top navigation with a progress indicator.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Oversized statistics as visual anchors (10 ACRES / 9,545 UNITS NEEDED / $5.78B GDP)
2. Thick black horizontal rules as section dividers
3. Pull quotes in large italic type

**Interaction Philosophy:** Minimal animation — this design communicates through stillness and confidence. Subtle hover underlines on links; smooth scroll only.

**Animation:** Headline words stagger in on load. Statistics count up on scroll entry. No parallax — this design is about precision, not movement.

**Typography System:** Bebas Neue for oversized display; IBM Plex Serif for body; IBM Plex Mono for data.

## Idea 3: "Golden Isles Prestige" — Coastal Luxury Investment Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Luxury coastal real estate meets institutional investment — inspired by JLL and CBRE premium offering memoranda, with a Golden Isles coastal warmth.

**Core Principles:**
1. Warmth within authority — warm stone and coastal sage tones prevent the cold institutional feel
2. Photography-led — aerial imagery is the hero; the site frames the land as the asset
3. Layered information architecture — progressive disclosure from overview to detail
4. Premium materiality — subtle texture, refined shadows, and glass-morphism accents

**Color Philosophy:** Deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) for text; warm stone (#E8E0D0) for section backgrounds; coastal sage (#4A6741) as the primary accent; PIER orange (#CB521E) for CTAs only. This palette evokes the Georgia coastal landscape — marshes, live oaks, and tidal creeks.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed aerial hero with a floating property summary card (glass-morphism). Content flows in alternating full-width and contained sections. A fixed right-side "Request Offering Memorandum" CTA panel follows the user.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Aerial photography with subtle vignette and coordinate overlay
2. Coastal sage accent bars on section headers
3. Glass-morphism cards for key statistics

**Interaction Philosophy:** Generous hover states on all interactive elements. Smooth parallax on hero. Section transitions use a subtle slide-up with fade.

**Animation:** Hero aerial zooms in slowly on load (Ken Burns effect). Statistics animate on scroll entry. Section headers slide in from the left.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (light/bold) for headlines; Lato for body; Lato Condensed for labels and captions.

## Selected Design: Idea 3 — "Golden Isles Prestige"

This approach best serves the dual audience of institutional investors and local government/nonprofit buyers. The warmth of the coastal palette humanizes what could feel like a cold investment document, while the photography-led layout immediately communicates the scale and potential of the site. The PIER orange CTA maintains brand consistency.
